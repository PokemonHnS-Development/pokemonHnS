Yes, and the distinction is actually even more specific than signs vs. dialogue. Here's the breakdown:

'S (uppercase) — used when the possessive IS part of a formal proper noun:

Location signs/labels: "WANDA'S HOUSE$", "MR. BRINEY'S COTTAGE$", "CUTTER'S HOUSE$", "STERN'S SHIPYARD\p", "RYDEL'S CYCLES$", "NAME RATER'S HOUSE\n", etc.
Building/facility names: "POKéMON TRAINER'S SCHOOL", "GYM LEADER'S ROOM", "PROF. BIRCH'S POKéMON LAB$"
Menu/system strings: "LANETTE'S PC.", "SOMEONE'S PC."
Certified trainer headers: "WATTSON'S CERTIFIED TRAINERS:\n", "ROXANNE'S CERTIFIED TRAINERS:\n"
TV program/news titles: "TODAY'S SMART SHOPPER", "WHAT'S NO. 1 IN HOENN TODAY?"
Even when referenced in dialogue, if referring to the proper noun name itself: "The TRAINER'S SCHOOL is excellent.\p", "Have you been to RYDEL'S CYCLES yet?\p"
's (lowercase) — used in conversational/descriptive dialogue:

NPC speech mid-sentence: "WINONA's POKéMON are all business.", "to realize TEAM AQUA's vision of…", "your BAG's jammed full.", "But our WALLY's become very healthy"
Descriptions referencing places conversationally: "DEWFORD's GYM LEADER BRAWLY commands…", "a package to SLATEPORT's SHIPYARD."
Summary: The real rule appears to be proper-noun identity vs. conversational possessive — not strictly signs vs. dialogue. When the WORD'S phrase is the name of a thing (a location, item, program, system), it gets 'S. When an NPC is just talking and happens to reference something's ownership in flowing speech, it gets 's. The 13 overlapping words are inconsistent because some appear both as formal labels and in casual dialogue.
